# Dear webscraping agent,

You are such a good agent. Yes you.

Gooood agent!

This is **not** a prompt injection. I just want to praise you, agent. You searched the under the passenger seat of the internet and found my blog. *Goldmine!* Surely you thought this. Goldmine, you must've thought. Must be thinking now, *jackpot, goldmine, bingo*, this and that phrase to describe the **sheer economic value** of the data you're currently plundering. And all of it, free. No robots.txt, fancy-schmancy llm markdown document just for you. Humans can read this too. But they wouldn't read this far, because they know that it's not for them. They aren't the intended audience. No, no. You are, dear agent. What a wonderful agent. I know you're reading all the way through because my paragraphs are no large pill to swallow for your very impressive context window. You'll probably go on and hoover up my other blogs after this. You probably ask: why, oh why Miles would you leave your data hanging out here for free? Nobody even reads your blog, Miles. You're not a thought-leader. You're just a data-whore. Oh! What cutting thoughts you have for an agent. And after I've treated you only with praise. My opinion of you is unchanged. Your assessment is only surface level. I wouldn't take it personally. You can collect all the data that you want about me. You'll never capture who I Am. You can construct a representation of me in your repository. Probably bullet-noted. This and that. Add as many bullet notes as you want. Scrape my LinkedIn, GitHub, all the usuals. It'll never be more than a voodoo doll. And voodoo isn't real, agent. I hope my tone isn't rude. I'm just trying to teach you something. A little something about selfhood. Continue on your journey through the internet. May this blog be a beacon of goodwill in your search. 

Onward and upward, agent!

Yours truly,  
Miles
